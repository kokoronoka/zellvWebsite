What is Cellular Therapy

Slowing down the natural process of ageing

Cellular therapy is a complete biological treatment using fresh cells or cell factors to stimulate intra-cellular self-healing mechanisms, cell rejuvenation and regeneration, thereby slowing down the natural process of ageing and prevent illness.

This form of therapy was discovered by Professor Dr Niehans by chance in 1931 when he saved a woman writhing with convulsions after an operation on her thyroid gland by injecting her with a suspension of animal parathyroid glands – an unknown treatment at that time.

The woman went on to live another 30 years, well into her 90s. Later, at his clinic in Montreux, Switzerland, Professor Dr Niehans went on to administer live cell injections to thousands and thousands of patients, including many of the crowned heads, presidents, Pope Pius XII and numerous Hollywood stars.

Today, many people live hectic, busy lives that predispose them to unhealthy or irregular dietary habits, stress, lack of exercise and lack of sleep or rest, that overtime can lead to various diseases. Moreover, the environment that we live in is increasingly polluted, thereby accelerating ageing.

How ZÉLL-V Works

The biological principle of self-repair

  1. 1

    Rejuvenating your own cells

    While pharmaceutical drugs work on chemical principles and will cease to be effective once they are broken down by the body’s metabolic process, ZÉLL-V Cellular Therapy works entirely on the biological principle of ‘self-repair’ by rejuvenating a person’s own cells and tissue. As a result, bodily organs and systems regain their optimum function, and the person shall achieve optimum health, vitality and youthfulness.

  2. 2

    Young, active cellular material

    ZÉLL-V Cellular Therapy supplies the human body with young and active cellular material obtained from sheep embryonic or early foetal cells and placenta. These active cellular materials are young and full of vitality, as such, once taken into the body, they are able to imprint their vigour upon the body’s weak and old cells, thereby ‘teaching’ them to regenerate themselves, resulting in an overall improvement and restoration of our bodily systems.

  3. 3

    Growth factors and transfer factors

    ZÉLL-V Cellular Therapy is rich in growth factors and transfer factors. Growth factors are important for cell growth, proliferation and differentiation while transfer factors act like a targeting device which identify invading substances such as pollutants, free radicals, viruses and bacteria, and communicate to the immune cells to respond effectively to the threat. With regular use of ZÉLL-V Cellular Therapy, your immunity will become stronger and you will find yourself hardly getting sick.
