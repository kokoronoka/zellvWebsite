Leading the Way in Cellular Therapy

Activating the body’s natural regenerative potential

With over two decades of expertise in anti-ageing and regenerative wellness, ZÉLL-V is a pioneer in cellular therapy. Founded by Dato’ Sharon Foong, our brand empowers individuals to activate the body’s natural regenerative potential — going beyond conventional medicine to restore health at the cellular level.

Powered by Swiss and German scientific expertise, ZÉLL-V has developed proprietary supplements and therapies that support healthy ageing, vitality, and set new standards in regenerative health. In 2016, the launch of the ZÉLL-V Wellness Hub marked a major milestone — earning recognition as Asia’s premier one-stop rejuvenation centre.

A Vision for Lasting Health

Health is our greatest wealth

At ZÉLL-V, we believe health is our greatest wealth. Through extensive research and collaboration with global experts, we have learnt that lasting health cannot be achieved through a single product or technique alone. True health requires a comprehensive approach to address the root causes of issues like fatigue, hormone imbalances, sub-optimal and chronic conditions.

Our vision is to empower individuals to take control of their health with personalised, regenerative solutions that restore balance and vitality. By prioritising cellular health, we help individuals unlock their full potential for long-term, sustainable wellness.

Globally Established Anti-ageing Brand

Accredited by international medical associations

ZÉLL-V is accredited by the International Association for Organ and Stem Cell Therapy Specialists Switzerland and Germany (IAOSS) and the German-French Society for Thymus Therapy (GFSTT), which has 80 years of history, giving customers full assurance of its superb quality and efficacy.

A medical advisory board consisting of international and local medical doctors as well as scientists forms a strong core of support for ZÉLL-V. Over the years, our exceptional quality, services, and credentials have earned us strong recognition and trust from medical and wellness institutions, associations, and organisations worldwide.

ZÉLL-V Wellness Hub

A one-stop rejuvenation centre

The establishment of the ZÉLL-V Wellness Hub in the heart of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, marks a significant milestone for Ruyi Groups of Companies, in line with its mission to help individuals achieve true health and wellness—both physically and mentally.

Guided by the slogan “Inspiring Better Health,” ZÉLL-V Wellness promotes a proactive approach to well-being, focusing on daily health maintenance and prevention rather than medical intervention.

The founder of ZÉLL-V, Dato’ Sharon Foong, embarked on a personal journey across countries such as Germany, Switzerland, and New Zealand in search of optimal health solutions. Through her insights and collaborations with international research teams, she realised that achieving lifelong wellness requires a holistic and multi-faceted approach. This led to the creation of the ZÉLL-V Wellness Hub, designed to support individuals on their path to complete well-being through education, screening, personalised care, and ongoing health planning.
