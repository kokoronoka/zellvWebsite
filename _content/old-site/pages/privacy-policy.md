### **6. Use of Cookies**

6.1 When you visit our website or use our services through our web portal, our web portal may create a “cookie”. A “cookie” is a piece of information stored on our hard drive containing information of your computer. The “cookie” enables our system to recognise your browser and remember your name and billing information so that you won’t have to retype it on your next visit.

6.2 A session ID cookie expires when you close your web browser. A persistent cookie remains on your hard drive for an extended period of time. You can remove the persistent cookie by following directions provided in your web browser’s “help” option.
