How To Boost Brain Health As You Get Older?

25 September 20235 min read

An investment in keeping the brain in desirable condition as we live longer is a precious bet towards our health. Our mental capacity, including the aspects of memory, knowledge and thinking, plays an important role in our living standards. Nevertheless, over time (year after year), it is generally expected that the cognitive functions will change and become sluggish. The positive aspect is that it is possible to preserve brain health with health supplements to complement active strategies of maintaining brain health during the ageing process.

The following are some of the important pieces of advice you can use to boost the vigour of your mind so that you have full and active mental capabilities to navigate you through life in its later years.

1. Consume Good Food

Like a symphony can only be played with a well-balanced arrangement of instruments, our bodies will only be in top shape when a variety of nutrients are eaten in a balanced diet. It is possible to consume a balanced and nutritious diet that can give the basic building blocks required to stimulate the cognitive process. Think of the food pyramid as your food map, where each level pertains to the various categories of components that can help in enhancing the wellness of your brain condition.

• Complex Carbohydrates and Grains are vital in giving the body the much-needed energy to fuel both the body and the mind. Foods such as quinoa, brown rice and whole wheat bread provide a slower release of energy that keeps the blood sugar level stable and maintains a regular supply of fuel to brain cells.

• Vegetables and Fruits are packed with antioxidants, vitamins and minerals essential for healthy cognitive function. They protect our brain against oxidative stress and strengthen our brain. Leafy greens, bell peppers, berries, and citrus fruits are a great choice, rich in vitamins and antioxidants.

• Proteins and Dairy are good protein sources with amino acids that form neurotransmitters- chemical messengers which help in brain cell communication. The addition of lean meats, poultry, fish, eggs and dairy products contributes to optimum brain health by assisting in the production and processing of neurotransmitters.

• Healthy Fats such as omega-3 fatty acids, in salmon, nuts, seeds and olive oil, play an important role in maintaining normal brain structure and function. Healthy fats support normal cell function and are associated with overall brain wellbeing.

The combination of complex carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins and minerals constitutes a balanced diet that supports your cognitive functioning ability. Instead of giving intense emphasis on single nutritional components or food groups, the key is to create a variety of tastes and nutrients, which can help you maintain your general health.

2. Stimulate Your Brain

After all, keeping your body fit with sports activities, why not keep your mind fit with thought-provoking activities in old age as well? The brain is adaptable throughout life, and by challenging the brain with new things, it may help keep your mind active and engaged. The following are some of the ways you can do that.

• Learn Something New – It can be a musical instrument, a new language, a hobby like painting, doing something new is physically challenging, and can support lifelong learning.

• Puzzles and Games like crosswords, sudoku, puzzles and chess are very stimulating to the brain as they involve problem solving, critical thinking and recalling.

• Lifelong Learning – think about online coursework or participating in workshops to keep learning and to broaden your knowledge base.

3. Load up on Your Nutrients

This is where providing your brain with the proper nutrition is just like building a good foundation for a house. Complex biochemical reactions in your brain depend on a wide variety of vitamins, minerals, and other compounds to run most effectively. The following elements are vital, and adding them to your diet using food and health supplements can go a long way in keeping your cognitive health when you reach old age.

• Resveratrol – A polyphenol found in berries, red wine and grapes, which helps promote robust blood flow to the brain. Besides that, its primary function as an antioxidant helps combat inflammation and oxidative stress. These mechanisms collectively contribute to its ability to slow cognitive decline and improve overall brain health.

• Astaxanthin – A powerful antioxidant naturally derived from microalgae, and its unique structure allow it to cross the blood-brain barrier and protect brain cells against oxidative damage. Its unique properties help to enhance the level of its cognition and memory preservation.

• Memophenol – A patented mixture extracted from wild blueberries and French grapes, naturally rich in polyphenols. Research has explored its potential in supporting mental performance and overall cognitive wellness. Scientifically proven to support cognition with a demonstrated bioavailability.

• Minerals – The important minerals, which will help in supporting overall brain performance, are magnesium, zinc, and iron. Magnesium is naturally found in nuts, seeds and leafy greens, and plays a crucial role in the production of neurotransmitters. Zinc and iron are essential for memory formation and attention, which help achieve overall cognitive vitality.

• Vitamin D – Vitamin D is also a very important vitamin for overall health and cognitive wellbeing. Exposure to sunlight and food like fatty fish, fortified dairy products, and eggs helps support healthy vitamin D status. Vitamin D is recognised for overcoming issues with neurotransmitters and enhancing neuroplasticity, which is the capability of the brain to change and learn.

• B Vitamins – The B vitamins, particularly the B6, B12 and folic acid, are required to decrease levels of homocysteine that are linked to the decline in cognition. Good sources of such essential nutrients are leafy greens, legumes, lean meats, and fortified cereals.

As we grow older, it is always brain health that needs priority. You can keep your cognitive functions in good condition, live your life to the fullest and maintain or enhance your cognitive functions by adhering to a balanced combination of proper diet, mental exercise and adequate nutrient intake.
