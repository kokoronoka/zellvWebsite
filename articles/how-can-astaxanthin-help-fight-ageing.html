How Can Astaxanthin Help Fight Ageing?

7 November 20233 min read

As people live longer, many are looking for ways to stay energetic, healthy, and confident in how they age. Astaxanthin, a naturally occurring substance found in red algae, salmon, shrimps and crabs, is seen as a hopeful answer to help reverse ageing signs. With its versatile benefits, this strong antioxidant proves to be an important complement in anti-ageing supplements and a major contributor to physical health, well-being and fitness.

So, what are all these incredible qualities of astaxanthin, and how can it help combat ageing?

1. Astaxanthin Properties

The bright pink colour of salmon and flamingos has one remarkable carotenoid pigment, Astaxanthin. Astaxanthin, commonly referred to as the King of Carotenoids, has been demonstrated to be more powerful than well-known antioxidants, including vitamin C, lutein and beta-carotene. In fact, research shows that astaxanthin is nearly 6,000 times more potent than the common antioxidant vitamin C, making it capable of neutralising the harmful free radicals.

Even more interesting, you only need a small amount to experience its benefits. Studies indicate that just 4mg of astaxanthin per day can bring major health benefits. This makes it a convenient and efficient option to include a potent antioxidant in your daily life.

The other notable characteristic of this compound is its ability to neutralise free radicals. As we age, our bodies tend to accumulate oxidative stress due to the increasing formation of free radicals, which break down our cells, proteins, and DNA. As time passes, this damage accelerates and predisposes a person to chronic illnesses, such as cardiovascular disease, neurodegenerative disorders and cancer. In its ability to curb oxidative stress, astaxanthin not only helps to slow invisible signs of ageing, e.g. wrinkles, age spots, etc., but also protects against underlying cellular damage that contributes to age-related conditions.

2. The Effect of Astaxanthin on Cognitive Function

Ageing is a complex process, not only manifested by wrinkles and physical appearances. Cognitive decline, such as memory loss and reduced mental sharpness, is one of the most serious concerns linked to old age. Luckily, astaxanthin, as an excellent antioxidant, shows significant promise in promoting brain health and cognitive function.

One of the key reasons for its effectiveness is its ability to cross the blood-brain barrier. This protective barrier shields the brain from harmful substances, but also inhibits the absorption of beneficial compounds. Because of its unique molecular structure, astaxanthin can pass through this barrier and directly reach brain cells, where it protects these important cells against oxidative damage.

The compound also possesses anti-inflammatory properties, which are especially beneficial to the brain. Chronic brain inflammation is known to contribute to cognitive decline and neurodegenerative diseases, such as Alzheimer’s and Parkinson’s diseases. Its anti-inflammatory property can also reduce inflammation and offer an additional protective layer against these debilitating conditions.

3. Astaxanthin to Have Better Body Health

As time goes on through our lives, our bodies are suffering quite a bit of wear and tear, often leading to issues such as joint pain, muscle fatigue, and a loss of endurance. Astaxanthin has an outstanding advantage of being able to minimise inflammation and counter oxidative stress in our body. These two causes are major culprits in the formation of chronic diseases, including arthritis and cardiovascular diseases, which increase in prevalence with age. This potent effect to reduce inflammation and antioxidant activity, in turn, has a positive impact on joint health and reduces the likelihood of such age-related issues.

It has also been demonstrated that astaxanthin can enhance endurance besides reducing body fatigue. It aids in the recovery of muscles after exercise, and therefore, it can be argued that it is a good supplement to provide to anyone who wishes to be physically active, regardless of his or her age. This can be a supportive nutrient for older adults who desire to stay active. They are able to return to physical activities sooner with greater ease.

Though ageing is a natural process, astaxanthin can help you age gracefully by supporting both physical and mental health. It can reduce free radicals, inflammation, and inhibit oxidation; an accomplishment that makes it a rare standout amongst other ingredients in the fight against the challenges of old age.
