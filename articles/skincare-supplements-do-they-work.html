Skincare Supplements. Do They Work?

17 January 20244 min read

Think of your skin as a complex biological engine. It won’t run optimally on topical fuel alone. Achieving naturally healthy skin requires targeted, internal nourishment that regenerates fibroblast cells and shields tissues from the molecules that accelerate ageing. We’re opening the vault on three powerful supplements that supply these missing nutrients. Discover how sheep placenta, AFA blue-green algae and anti-inflammatories like Zinc and Astaxanthin reset your complexion from the core.

1. How Sheep Placenta Benefits the Skin

Sheep placenta is one of the most interesting skincare supplements that has gained popularity over the past years. The sheep placenta is rich in proteins, vitamins, minerals, and bioactive compounds,and it is widely used to support skin vitality and a healthy looking complexion. The skin contains fibroblast cells, which are responsible for producing collagen and hyaluronic acid, key components for healthy skin.

Sheep placenta extracts contain nutrients that revitalise these fibroblast cells, bringing them back to their optimum efficiency,promoting the natural production of collagen and hyaluronic acid. Hyaluronic acid plays a key role in retaining moisture and keeping the skin supple, while collagen supports skin firmness and elasticity. Both are essential for a smooth, youthful-looking complexion.

Sheep placenta extract also contributes nutrients that support skin hydration. Healthy skin also depends on proper hydration, as it ensures the skin is neither too wet nor too dry, and helps eliminate dry, flaky skin. Regular use of sheep placenta supplements promotes a well-hydrated skin and a youthful appearance, making it an attractive addition to your skincare routine.

2. Blue-Green Algae Has Powerful Antioxidants

Blue-green algae, which include Aphanizomenon Flos-Aquae (AFA), are nutritionally rich superfoods that comprise a colourful array of vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants. Add to this the presence of mighty C-Phycocyanin, a natural antioxidant 20 times stronger than vitamin C, which combines to strengthen cell and tissue resistance to oxidative stress, or the harmful free radicals capable of causing premature ageing effects and damage skin cells. In the case of blue-green algae supplements, you present strong antioxidants in your body, which help protect your skin against oxidative stress and early ageing.

In addition, these microalgae contain a high concentration of chlorophyll, a natural body cleanser that promotes healthy and radiant skin. Through detoxification, chlorophyll helps to give you a healthier complexion, which minimises your risk of developing skin problems, such as breakouts or acne.

3. Organic Nutrients That Lower Inflammation And Associated Disorders

Some of the causes of various skin conditions include inflammation, a major underlying factor in conditions such as acne and hyperpigmentation. The inclusion of organic nutrients, such as Astaxanthin, green tea extract, and zinc, can be a win-win for your skin.

Astaxanthin, the bright pink pigment found in salmon and flamingoes, has become recognised as a powerful anti-inflammatory agent, primarily due to its role in the microalgae from which it originated. The neutralisation of free radicals and its anti-inflammatory capabilities, which Astaxanthin demonstrates, make it a valuable addition to your skincare regimen, leaving a wide range of other antioxidants behind.

The intake of Astaxanthin can help alleviate redness, swelling, and irritation on the skin, making the person calmer and less likely to develop inflammatory skin diseases. Findings also indicated that besides reducing wrinkles, the utilisation of Astaxanthin prevents the generation of age spots and also protects the fibroblasts against the harmful effects of free radicals.

Green tea extract is another natural beauty product composed of polyphenols, which also possess amazing anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Such properties are beneficial in combating acne, as they inhibit inflammation and help control the bacteria that cause acne. The prolonged intake consisting of green tea extract supplements will contribute to the preservation of healthy and clear skin; therefore, for a person inclined to acne outbursts, it is a wonderful suggestion.

Zinc is essential for overall health and serves as a crucial facilitator in the inflammatory process of the skin, also aiding in the innate healing of the skin. Zinc deficiency makes the skin vulnerable to various issues, including acne and hyperpigmentation. With zinc supplements in your diet, you are providing your skin with the support it needs to combat inflammation and achieve a more even complexion.

To optimise your skincare supplements for your skin, choose them wisely and incorporate them into a balanced diet. Sheep placenta features a collagen booster, while blue-green algae serves as a powerful antioxidant. Astaxanthin, green tea extract, and zinc are responsible for the anti-inflammatory properties. These supplements provide nutrients to your skin from the inside out.
