So, What’s Going On Inside Our Bodies?

Here’s the thing. We are in constant operation—digesting, healing, detoxifying—our bodies are doing it all the time. But every day, we’re exposed to things that throw it off balance. Air pollution, fried food, cigarette smoke, extended exposure to the sun, all this forms free radicals.

Free radicals are unstable molecules with an unpaired electron, and they are highly reactive, damaging healthy cells by stealing electrons from stable molecules in the body, which harms cell membranes, proteins, and DNA. As free radical damage accumulates, the body’s natural defence systems, including antioxidants and repair enzymes, struggle to keep up with and repair the damage. This buildup of oxidative stress is widely linked to the signs of premature ageing, persistent inflammation, and the development of serious illnesses like heart disease and cancer.

Antioxidants are where it comes in. They’re like internal peacekeepers. They assist in neutralising free radicals and maintain the balance of your body. The only problem? Most of us aren’t getting enough through diet alone.
