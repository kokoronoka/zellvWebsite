The popularity gained by cellular therapy can be explained by the fact that this approach makes use of the natural processes of the human body. This follows the current trend towards personal and patient-centred medical practices that involve treatments that are specific to the unique biology of an individual. Such versatility opens up any number of therapeutic possibilities, and cellular therapy can be considered a multifunctional avenue onto which any number of health concerns can be addressed.

In addition to supporting the skin’s natural defences against the effects of ageing, sheep placenta cells also promote overall cell repair and rejuvenation throughout the body. Cellular health is essential to our being, including the brain cells, muscle cells, bone cells, and red blood cells. Cell therapy initiates the process of self-healing by the body at the cellular level, going beyond skin enhancement to beneficial effects such as improved sleeping quality, better blood circulation.

Cellular therapy does not present itself as a replacement option to traditional pharmaceuticals; on the contrary, it is considered a supplement as part of the healthcare system. The combined approach of cellular therapy and pharmaceuticals enables an all-round treatment approach where the immediate relief of symptoms and the underlying cause are considered, and the strength of each is utilised.

Pharmaceuticals manage acute conditions, address the symptoms, and give immediate relief, whereas the cell therapy products play long-term roles involving healing and regeneration.
