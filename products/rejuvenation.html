What Is Rejuvenation?

Renewing the body at the cellular level

As we age, our cells renew themselves more slowly, produce less energy and gather damage over time. The body becomes less able to repair its tissues, which shows up as fatigue, slower recovery, weaker immunity and visible signs of ageing.

Cellular rejuvenation works with the body’s own regenerative potential. It is the idea behind cellular therapy, discovered by Professor Dr Niehans in 1931: supplying cells with bioactive materials that help them repair, renew and function like younger cells.

Sheep placenta is one of the richest natural sources of these materials. Revered for centuries in Traditional Chinese Medicine, it is packed with amino acids, proteins and growth factors that support cell renewal, energy and overall vitality.
